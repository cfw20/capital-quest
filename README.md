# Capital Quest

A kid-friendly game for learning world capitals. There is a 67-level campaign across five worlds, plus coins, gems, upgrades, and a shop. Every country still has a study card with a map, pronunciation, a memory hook, and a useful fact.

## Play online

https://cfw20.github.io/capital-quest/

## Play on a computer

1. Unzip this folder (or copy `index.html`, `data.js`, and `levels.js` together).
2. Open `index.html` in a browser.
3. Keep all three files in the same folder.

## How to play

- **Choose a level** to jump to any of the 67 levels — nothing is locked. **Continue** still takes you to the first level you have not starred yet.
- Early levels show the study card first, then ask you to type the capital. Later worlds get harder: mixed countries, no hints, timers, one try, and strict spelling.
- Score well to earn 1–3 stars, coins, and gems. Three-star runs, bosses, and world chests are the main way to earn gems.
- Spend coins and gems in **Shop & upgrades**. There are help items and bundles, ranked upgrades, themes, mascots, auras, card and map styles, titles, celebrations, and sound packs.
- Beat a world’s boss to open a chest with bonus coins, gems, and an unlock.
- **Free practice** is the old sandbox if you just want to revise a pack or region.

Close spellings still count, except on strict levels (unless you have bought Lucky Pencil). Famous cities that aren’t the capital get a gentle hint. Your progress is saved in the browser.
